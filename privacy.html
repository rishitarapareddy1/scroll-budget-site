Privacy Policy

Scrollgate for Android and iOS · Effective November 2, 2026

In short: everything Scrollgate knows about you stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and we never receive your data.

What Scrollgate does

Scrollgate gives you a daily budget for apps you choose to limit: swipes on Android, and opens or minutes on iOS. When the budget runs out, those apps are blocked until your daily reset. You can name an accountability buddy who gets a text, which you send yourself, when you loosen your limits or turn off the app.

On Android: Accessibility Service

Scrollgate uses Android's Accessibility Service API. This is how it counts swipes and shows the "budget spent" and pause screens. The service is configured to receive only two kinds of information:

  • which app is currently in the foreground, and
  • that a scroll happened in one of the apps you chose to limit.

The service is configured without access to your screen's contents, and Scrollgate doesn't read or keep any text, images, messages, passwords or anything you type. It doesn't record or store which apps you open, only a running swipe count for the current day. You turn the service on yourself in Android settings, and you can turn it off at any time. If you do, Scrollgate notes it in its on-device accountability log.

On iOS: Screen Time

On iPhone, Scrollgate uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls). You choose apps to limit in Apple's own picker. Scrollgate receives only anonymous tokens for your choices, not the names of the apps, and can't see what you do inside any app. iOS tells Scrollgate when you've used the chosen apps for a set number of minutes, and shows Scrollgate's lock screen over them. You grant Screen Time access yourself, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time. If you do, Scrollgate notes it in its on-device accountability log the next time you open it.

Information stored on your device

Scrollgate stores the following, only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • the apps you chose to limit;
  • your daily budget, reset time and other limit settings, and how much of today's budget is left;
  • on iOS, the anonymous app tokens from Apple's picker;
  • your accountability buddy's name and phone number, if you add one;
  • a salted, one-way hash of your settings PIN (never the PIN itself);
  • an accountability log of when limits were used up, loosened or turned off;
  • the most recent network time reading (see below).

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. On Android it's excluded from cloud backups. On iPhone, like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the device.

Network use

So that changing your phone's clock can't reset your budget early, Scrollgate checks the current time online. On Android it uses Google's time services (Android's network time, time.google.com, or the date header from www.google.com). On iOS it reads the date header from www.apple.com. These requests contain no personal information. As with any internet request, the server sees your device's IP address, under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. Scrollgate makes no other network requests.

Text messages to your buddy

Scrollgate never sends text messages itself. When something your buddy should know about happens, it opens your own messaging app with a pre-written message and your buddy's number filled in. You decide whether to send it. Any message you send goes through your messaging app and carrier, and Scrollgate never sees its contents. On iOS, the system tells Scrollgate whether you sent or cancelled the text, which it records only in its on-device log.

Sharing

We don't sell, share or transfer any data, because we never receive any. There are no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting in the app.

Deleting your data

On Android, uninstall Scrollgate, or clear its storage in Settings → Apps → Scrollgate → Storage. On iPhone, delete the app. Either removes everything listed above from the device.

Children

Scrollgate isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
